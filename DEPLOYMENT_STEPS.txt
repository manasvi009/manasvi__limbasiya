TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK FIXES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Problem: "Environment variable error"
Solution: Redeploy after adding environment variables
         Go to Deployments → Click latest → "Redeploy"

Problem: "MongoDB connection failed"
Solution: Check MongoDB URI is correct
         Try connecting from MongoDB Atlas Connection Status

Problem: "Site not loading after deployment"
Solution: Wait 1-2 minutes for cold start
         Refresh page (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
         Clear browser cache

Problem: "Authentication not working"
Solution: Check NEXTAUTH_URL and NEXTAUTH_SECRET are set
         Verify they match exactly (case-sensitive)
         Redeploy
